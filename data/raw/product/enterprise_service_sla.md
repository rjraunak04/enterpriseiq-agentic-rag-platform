# Enterprise Service SLA

## 1. Purpose

This document defines internal service targets for NexaCorp's Enterprise platform.

## 2. Priority 1

A Priority 1 incident represents a complete production outage or critical loss of service for a significant customer population.

The target initial response time for Priority 1 incidents is 15 minutes.

## 3. Priority 2

A Priority 2 incident represents substantial degradation with a viable workaround unavailable or severely limited.

The target initial response time for Priority 2 incidents is one hour.

## 4. Priority 3

A Priority 3 incident represents limited impact or a non-critical defect.

The target initial response time for Priority 3 incidents is four business hours.

## 5. Availability Objective

The monthly availability objective for the Enterprise production service is 99.9 percent, excluding approved maintenance windows.

## 6. Escalation

Priority 1 incidents require immediate escalation to the on-call incident lead.
