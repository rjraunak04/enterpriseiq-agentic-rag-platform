# IT Incident Reporting Procedure

## 1. Scope

This procedure covers operational IT incidents such as service outages, device failures, and application-access problems.

## 2. Reporting

Employees should report operational IT incidents through the IT Service Desk.

## 3. Critical Incidents

A widespread outage affecting a business-critical service should be marked as critical and escalated to the on-call IT operations lead.

## 4. Security Incidents

Suspected phishing, credential theft, malware, or unauthorized data access must follow the Information Security Policy SEC-001 rather than only the standard IT incident process.

## 5. Incident Information

Reports should include the affected service, observed symptoms, approximate start time, and known business impact.
