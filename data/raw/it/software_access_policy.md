# Software Access Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs access to company-managed software and SaaS applications.

## 2. Access Principle

Access is granted according to job responsibilities and the principle of least privilege.

## 3. Approval

Standard applications may be approved by the employee's manager. Privileged or administrative access requires approval from the system owner.

## 4. Review

Privileged access is reviewed at least quarterly.

## 5. Termination

Access must be removed when it is no longer required and must be revoked promptly following employment termination.
