# VPN Access Guide

## 1. Purpose

NexaCorp's VPN provides secure remote access to approved internal services.

## 2. Requirements

VPN access requires a company-managed device, an active corporate account, and multi-factor authentication.

## 3. Connection

Employees should launch the approved NexaCorp VPN client, authenticate with their corporate account, and complete the multi-factor authentication challenge.

## 4. Access Problems

Users should first confirm internet connectivity and retry authentication. Repeated failures should be reported to the IT Service Desk.

## 5. Security

Employees must not share VPN credentials or approve an unexpected multi-factor authentication request.
