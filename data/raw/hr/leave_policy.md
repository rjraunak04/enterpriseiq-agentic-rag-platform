# Leave Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to full-time NexaCorp Technologies employees unless a local employment requirement provides greater benefits.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit planned leave at least five working days in advance when reasonably possible. Manager approval is required before planned leave begins.

## 3. Carry Forward

Up to five unused annual-leave days may be carried into the following calendar year. Carried leave should be used by March 31 unless People Operations approves an exception.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Medical documentation may be requested for an absence exceeding three consecutive working days.

## 5. Parental Leave

Eligible primary caregivers may receive up to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Eligible secondary caregivers may receive up to four weeks of paid parental leave, subject to applicable local requirements.

## 6. Exceptions

Policy exceptions require written approval from People Operations.
