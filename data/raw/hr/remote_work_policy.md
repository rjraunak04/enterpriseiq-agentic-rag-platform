# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

The remote-work program supports flexible working while preserving collaboration, security, and customer coverage.

## 2. Standard Eligibility

Employees who have completed 90 days of employment and whose roles are designated hybrid-eligible may request remote work.

## 3. Remote Work Limit

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to three days per workweek.

The employee's manager must approve the recurring remote-work schedule.

## 4. Office Requirements

Teams may designate collaboration days when in-person attendance is required. Business-critical meetings may override a normal remote-work day.

## 5. Security

Remote workers must use company-managed devices for restricted company information and must follow applicable IT and information-security requirements.

## 6. International Remote Work

Working from another country requires advance approval from People Operations, Information Security, and the employee's business leader.
