# Employee Handbook

## 1. Purpose

This handbook provides baseline employment guidance for NexaCorp Technologies employees. Where a dedicated policy exists, the dedicated policy takes precedence over this handbook.

## 2. Working Hours

The standard workweek is 40 hours, Monday through Friday. Teams may use flexible schedules with manager approval while maintaining required customer and operational coverage.

## 3. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests should normally be submitted through the HR system before the planned absence.

Detailed eligibility, carry-forward rules, and exceptions are governed by the Leave Policy (HR-002).

## 4. Remote Work

Eligible employees may participate in NexaCorp's hybrid working program. Detailed remote-work limits and approval requirements are defined in the Remote Work Policy (HR-003).

## 5. Business Travel

Employees travelling on company business must follow the Business Travel Policy (HR-004) and applicable Finance reimbursement requirements.

## 6. Policy Precedence

When this handbook conflicts with a dedicated policy, the latest effective version of the dedicated policy governs.
