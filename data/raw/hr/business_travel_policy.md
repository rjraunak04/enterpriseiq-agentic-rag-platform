# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for approved NexaCorp business travel.

## 2. Approval

Domestic business travel requires manager approval before booking.

International business travel requires approval from both the employee's manager and department director.

## 3. Booking

Employees should use NexaCorp's approved travel channel when available. Economy class is the standard for flights under six hours.

## 4. Expenses

Reasonable business travel expenses may be reimbursed when they comply with Finance Policy FIN-001.

## 5. Personal Extensions

Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons when approved, but incremental personal costs are not reimbursable.
