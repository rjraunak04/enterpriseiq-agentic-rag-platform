# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

Company technology resources are provided primarily for authorized business activities.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

Employees must not intentionally bypass security controls, install unauthorized software, share credentials, or use company systems for unlawful activity.

## 3. Limited Personal Use

Limited personal use is permitted when it does not interfere with work, create material cost, or violate company policy.

## 4. Monitoring

Company systems may be logged and monitored in accordance with applicable law and NexaCorp policy.

## 5. Reporting

Suspected misuse or security concerns should be reported through approved IT or Security channels.
