# Data Classification Policy

## 1. Purpose

NexaCorp classifies information according to its sensitivity and required handling controls.

## 2. Public

Public information is approved for unrestricted external distribution.

## 3. Internal

Internal information is intended for NexaCorp employees and authorized partners and should not be publicly distributed without approval.

## 4. Confidential

Confidential information includes sensitive business, customer, financial, and employee information. Access is limited to authorized users with a business need.

## 5. Restricted

Restricted information represents NexaCorp's highest sensitivity level and requires specifically authorized access and approved protection controls.

## 6. Handling

Users must apply storage, transmission, and access controls appropriate to the information's classification.
