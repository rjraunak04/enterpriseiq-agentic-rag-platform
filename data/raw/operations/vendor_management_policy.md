# Vendor Management Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes minimum controls for onboarding and managing third-party vendors.

## 2. Due Diligence

New vendors must complete business due diligence before onboarding.

Vendors that will access Confidential or Restricted information also require an information-security review.

## 3. Contract Approval

Contracts must be reviewed through the approved contracting process before signature.

## 4. Performance Review

Critical vendors are reviewed at least annually for performance, risk, and continuing business need.

## 5. Procurement

Purchasing activity must also comply with Procurement Policy FIN-002.

## 6. Offboarding

Vendor access to NexaCorp systems and information must be removed when the relationship ends.
