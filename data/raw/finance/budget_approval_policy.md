# Budget Approval Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines approval authority for unplanned business expenditure.

## 2. Manager Authority

Managers may approve eligible unplanned expenditure up to INR 50,000 when sufficient departmental budget is available.

## 3. Director Authority

Expenditure above INR 50,000 and up to INR 250,000 requires department-director approval.

## 4. Executive Approval

Expenditure above INR 250,000 requires approval from the relevant executive leader and Finance.

## 5. Budget Availability

Approval authority does not override budget availability, procurement requirements, or vendor-control requirements.
