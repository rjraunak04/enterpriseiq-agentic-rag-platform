# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy covers reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred on behalf of NexaCorp Technologies.

## 2. Submission Deadline

Expense claims should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the transaction date.

## 3. Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any individual expense of INR 1,500 or more.

## 4. Approval Thresholds

Claims up to INR 10,000 require the employee's manager approval.

Claims above INR 10,000 and up to INR 50,000 require department-head approval.

Claims above INR 50,000 require Finance Controller approval in addition to department-head approval.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal entertainment, traffic penalties, personal upgrades, and expenses without a legitimate business purpose are not reimbursable.

## 6. Travel Expenses

Approved business travel expenses must also comply with Business Travel Policy HR-004.
