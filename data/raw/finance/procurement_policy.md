# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines controls for purchasing goods and services for NexaCorp Technologies.

## 2. Purchase Requests

Business purchases above INR 25,000 require an approved purchase request before a supplier commitment is made.

## 3. Competitive Quotations

Purchases above INR 100,000 normally require at least three competitive quotations unless Strategic Procurement approves a documented exception.

## 4. Purchase Orders

A purchase order is required for supplier commitments above INR 50,000 unless the purchase belongs to an approved exempt category.

## 5. Conflict of Interest

Employees involved in supplier selection must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest.

## 6. Vendor Requirements

New suppliers must satisfy the Vendor Management Policy OPS-001 before onboarding.
